ArcSolar brings your customers, quotes, communications, installation work and payments together. These guides help owners configure the business, sales teams move customers forward and operations teams deliver the work.

## Meet ArgonixIntelligence

ArgonixIntelligence appears inside supported workflows: electricity-bill analysis, call summaries and dashboard briefs. It helps you understand information and decide what to do next. Availability depends on your plan, permissions and the data available.

Always check extracted bill values, call summaries and recommendations against their source. A summary does not approve finance, confirm a rebate, settle a payment or complete an installation.

The separate **ArgonixIntelligence** sidebar item may display **Soon**. Embedded tools can still be available. Each guide ends with a practical explanation of how intelligence fits that workflow.

## Read the screen labels

**Mock data**, **Fictional preview** and **Manual preview** identify demonstrations. Their values are examples; some actions do not save. Screenshots in these guides use fictional examples and may show these labels.

A missing menu can reflect your role or plan. A **Coming soon** label is not an instruction to connect a service. See [availability](/reference/availability).

The organisation onboarding guide follows the **qa** onboarding release. Other guides describe the inspected workflow; your organisation's enabled features and saved settings determine what appears.
