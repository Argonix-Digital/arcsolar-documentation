Use your manager-approved practice customer for this lesson.

## Read the record

1. Open **Customers → Customer Records** and search for the customer.
2. Check name, contact details, installation address and assigned rep.
3. Open **Activity** and read the latest conversation.
4. Find the pipeline stage, open tasks and any quote or project.

A stage summarises progress; the record explains it. Do not create a second customer because a record was hard to find.

## Try it

Tell your manager what the customer wants, what has already happened and what should happen next. Point to the record supporting your answer.

Keep [Customer records](/customers/records) and [Pipeline](/customers/pipeline) nearby when you begin live work.
