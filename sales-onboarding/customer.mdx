Use your practice customer.

## Open the record

1. Open [**Customers → Customer Records**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/customers).
2. Type the name, phone or email in **Search customer records...**.
3. Open the customer.

Always search before you add someone. Two records for one customer splits their history.

## Read it

Check:

- Name, phone, email and installation address.
- **Lead owner**: is it you?
- The latest calls, messages and notes in the activity.
- The pipeline stage, open tasks and any quote.

A new lead with no owner shows **Claim lead**. Select it to make the lead yours.

## Try it

Tell your manager in two sentences: what the customer wants, and what happens next.

Learn more: [Customer records](/customers/records) and [Pipeline](/customers/pipeline).
