Open [**Quick Create Quote**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/quotes/new) with your practice customer. Stop before sending.

## Build it

1. Choose the customer and check the address.
2. Choose the services: solar, battery or both.
3. Choose the exact equipment and quantities.
4. Check the savings, incentives, price and payment option.
5. Check **Review & BOM**.
6. Open the customer preview.

Step by step: [Create a quote](/quoting/walkthrough).

## Check three things

| Check | Ask yourself |
| --- | --- |
| **The system** | Do the models, quantities, phase and install work match the home? |
| **The incentives** | Is the price before or after STCs? Is each rebate counted once? See [Incentives and STCs](/quoting/incentives-stcs) |
| **The payments** | Do the deposit, delivery payment and installation balance add up to the total? See [Payment options](/quoting/payment-options) |

A finance repayment is an estimate, not an approval.

## Before your first real quote

Walk your manager through the preview. Get their OK before you send your first one. Once sent, a quote keeps its prices even if the catalogue changes.
