Use one practice customer to walk your manager through the work.

## Rehearse the essentials

- Find the customer, read the latest activity and explain the next step.
- Prepare a conversation and record a useful note and task.
- Find site uploads and review the bill inputs.
- Build a practice quote and check its customer preview.
- Explain the incentives, payment schedule and handover requirements.

Stop at the preview. Practice should not send messages, issue an offer or take a payment from a real customer.

## Know when to pause

Missing evidence, a mismatched system, uncertain consent or an unclear payment result needs review before proceeding. Ask your manager who approves pricing exceptions, technical questions and your first live quote.

## Start the day

Use **Rep Overview**, appointments and **My tasks**. Open the customer before acting, then leave a next step with an owner and date. [Your sales day](/customers/daily-routine) is the routine to keep nearby.
