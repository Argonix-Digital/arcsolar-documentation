## Show your manager

Using the practice customer, walk your manager through:

1. Finding the customer and saying what happens next.
2. A note and a task after a call.
3. The bill and photos, and the checked bill values.
4. A quote, up to the customer preview.
5. The incentives, payments and what you'd hand over.

## Know when to stop and ask

Ask your manager before going on if:

- The bill, photos or site details are missing.
- The system doesn't suit the home.
- You're not sure the customer agreed to be contacted.
- A payment result is unclear.
- A customer asks for a price you can't give.

## Your daily routine

1. Open **Rep Overview**. Check today's appointments and what's overdue.
2. Open **Tasks** and work through what's due.
3. Before you contact anyone, read their record.
4. After every contact, leave a note and a next step with a date.

Keep [Your sales day](/customers/daily-routine) handy.
