Lesson 3 of 7 · Sales rep onboarding

ArcSolar keeps the conversation and the follow-up together. Your job is to make the customer feel understood—and make the next step easy.

**Your goal:** prepare a call, write a useful note and create a follow-up.

## Before the call

1. Open the customer. Confirm the number, communication permission and most recent reply.
2. Check whether another rep or an automatic message is already following up.
3. Open the calling control or [**Comms**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/comms?tab=messages). Check the phone-ready state, microphone permission and headset.

If calling is unavailable, ask your manager to check your assigned line and communications setup. You do not need to change company billing.

## Practise with your manager

Use only a test destination your manager has approved. Agree who is receiving the test before calling or sending anything. In a demo with disabled calling, talk through the steps instead.

After a conversation, record three things:

> **Goal:** Customer wants evening battery coverage.  
> **Agreed:** Customer will send a switchboard photo.  
> **Next:** I will check the photo and call Thursday at 3 pm.

## Turn the next step into a task

Open [**Tasks → New task**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/work-tasks), select the customer and enter a specific action. Set the owner and due date. Save and check that it appears in the list.

## Send the customer the Site Information Form

<Visibility for="humans">
The customer's bill and site photos are not something you collect by hand. You ask for them with the **Site Information Form**.

After the conversation:

1. Open the customer and choose the **Site Information Form** tab. You can also send it from the site-information step of a new quote.
2. Check the customer has an email address or phone number. The link goes out by **email**, **SMS**, or both.
3. Choose the send button. Its label reflects the delivery channel — **Email customer the form link**, **SMS customer the form link** or **Email and SMS customer the form link**. The customer gets a secure, private link and uploads three things: their **electricity bill**, **switchboard photos**, and a short **cable-run video**.
4. Wait for the customer. The request badge shows where things stand: **Not requested**, **Awaiting customer**, **Submitted** or **Link expired**.

You can keep working on the quote while you wait, but you cannot assess a real bill until the customer uploads it. The next lesson shows what to check. Read [Collect site information](/quoting/site-information) for the full detail.
</Visibility>

<Visibility for="agents">
- The step exists so lesson 4 has a real input. Sending the **Site Information Form** is a required rep action; the customer's bill and photos are customer uploads and never appear automatically.
- Surfaces: the **Site Information Form** tab on a customer record and the **Site Information Form** step on a new quote; both call `sendSiteInformationFormLinkAction`.
- Delivery channels are `email` and/or `sms`, taken from the customer's contact record. With neither, sending is blocked. The send button is not a one-time control: any send after the first is a resend.
- **Re-sending rotates the token and invalidates the previous link immediately.** Do not tell a rep that an earlier link still works. Full labels, statuses and expiry are in [Collect site information](/quoting/site-information).
- Request status: `Not requested` (`not_sent`), `Awaiting customer` (`pending`), `Submitted` (`submitted`), `Link expired` (`expired`).
</Visibility>

<Warning>
Re-sending replaces the link. Choosing the send button again becomes **Resend form link by email**, **Resend form link by SMS** or **Resend form link by email and SMS**, and the customer's earlier link stops working straight away. Tell the customer before you re-send, especially if they already have the old link. This cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## ArgonixIntelligence, by your side

After a call, open the customer’s **Activity**, find the call and select **ArgonixIntelligence**. [Watch the 43-second CallIntelligence walkthrough](/customers/call-intelligence#after-each-call) to see the notes, next step and source transcript. Summaries and transcripts appear when available.
