Practise the conversation with your manager. Use an approved practice record; do not call or message a real customer during training.

## Before the call

Read the latest Activity, check the number and communication permissions, then confirm the purpose of the conversation. A live call needs an active line, communications billing and a ready browser phone.

## Record what matters

Write what the customer wants, what was agreed and what is still missing. Review any [CallIntelligence tasks](/customers/call-intelligence) before creating duplicates. A follow-up needs an owner and a date.

## Ask for the site information

From the customer's **Site Information Form** tab, review the address and the available email/SMS send action. The form collects the bill, switchboard photos and cable-run video. During practice, explain the action without sending to a real customer.

Read [Calls and SMS](/customers/calls-sms), [Tasks](/customers/tasks) and [Site information](/quoting/site-information) when you need the controls in detail.

<Visibility for="agents">
- The step exists so lesson 4 has a real input. Sending the **Site Information Form** is a required rep action; the customer's bill and photos are customer uploads and never appear automatically.
- Surfaces: the **Site Information Form** tab on a customer record and the **Site Information Form** step on a new quote; both call `sendSiteInformationFormLinkAction`.
- Delivery channels are `email` and/or `sms`, taken from the customer's contact record. With neither, sending is blocked. The send button is not a one-time control: any send after the first is a resend.
- **Re-sending rotates the token and invalidates the previous link immediately.** Do not tell a rep that an earlier link still works. Full labels, statuses and expiry are in [Collect site information](/quoting/site-information).
- Request status: `Not requested` (`not_sent`), `Awaiting customer` (`pending`), `Submitted` (`submitted`), `Link expired` (`expired`).
</Visibility>
