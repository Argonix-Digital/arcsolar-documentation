Practise with your manager on the practice customer. Don't contact a real customer yet.

## Before you call

1. Read the latest activity on the customer.
2. Check the phone number and that they're happy to be contacted.
3. Know why you're calling.

To call from ArcSolar you need a business number. Ask your manager if you don't have one. See [Calls and SMS](/customers/calls-sms).

## After you call

1. Write a short note: what they want, what you agreed, what's missing.
2. Check for tasks ArcSolar already made from the call. See [CallIntelligence](/customers/call-intelligence).
3. Add a task for the next step, with an owner and a due date. See [Tasks](/customers/tasks).

> Comparing battery options. Waiting on switchboard photos. Call Thursday after 3 pm.

## Ask for the bill and photos

On the customer's **Site Information Form** tab, send the form by email or SMS. The customer uploads their electricity bill, switchboard photos and a cable-run video.

Sending it again makes a new link. The old link stops working. See [Site information](/quoting/site-information).
