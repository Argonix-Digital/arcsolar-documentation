## Follow up

1. Open the quote and check whether it was sent, viewed or accepted.
2. Read the customer's latest reply.
3. Check the [scheduled follow-up messages](/quoting/follow-ups) before sending your own.
4. Check your tasks before adding new ones.

## Change a quote

Use a [quote revision](/quoting/revisions). Check the whole quote and the payments again before you publish it.

Once a quote is accepted or paid, you may not be able to change it. Ask your manager what to do.

## Hand over a sale

Accepted, paid and finance-approved are three different things. Check each one.

On the project, leave:

- The agreed system and price.
- Anything you promised the customer.
- Open questions.
- Who does what next.

A sale is not ready to install yet. Operations still check the equipment, installer and site. See [Projects and jobs](/delivery/projects).
