## Check what came back

Open the customer's **Site Information Form** tab. The status shows **Awaiting customer**, **Submitted** or **Link expired**.

Nothing uploaded yet? Send the form again and wait. Never guess the customer's usage. See [Site information](/quoting/site-information).

Check:

- The address, roof, number of storeys and phase.
- Existing solar or battery.
- The switchboard photos and cable-run video.

Write down anything an installer needs to confirm.

## Check the bill

1. In the quote builder, choose **Assess bills with ArgonixIntelligence**.
2. Compare the period, usage, rates and charges with the real bill.
3. Apply only the values you checked.

Got existing solar? The bill shows what they buy from the grid, not their total use. See [Bill analysis](/quoting/bill-analysis).

## Explain savings

Savings are an estimate. Say so. If you don't have enough information, ask for more before quoting. See [Savings estimates](/quoting/savings).
