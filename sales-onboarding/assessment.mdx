## Review the evidence

Check installation address, roof, storeys, electrical phases, existing equipment and access. Review the bill, switchboard photos and cable-run video. Record what still needs technical confirmation.

## Review a bill

Choose **Assess bill with ArgonixIntelligence**, then compare the suggested period, consumption, rates and charges with the source. Apply only reviewed values. [Bill analysis](/quoting/bill-analysis) explains common errors.

## Explain the savings inputs

Confirm whether consumption means total household use or grid purchases. Existing solar changes the meaning of a bill's imports. Review use patterns, system assumptions and the available evidence.

Explain the result as an estimate. If inputs are insufficient, ask for better evidence or use the omit-savings option where offered. See [Savings estimates](/quoting/savings).

- **Precondition for the assessment step below:** a real uploaded bill, collected by the **Site Information Form** in [lesson 3](/sales-onboarding/conversation). Nothing is fetched automatically and no lesson creates the bill.
- Read the request status before assessing: `Not requested` (`not_sent`), `Awaiting customer` (`pending`), `Submitted` (`submitted`), `Link expired` (`expired`). Only a genuine customer upload gives genuine values.
- With nothing uploaded, the correct action is to send or re-send the form and wait — never to proceed on assumed or sample values as if they were the customer's. See [Collect site information](/quoting/site-information).
