Lesson 4 of 7 · Sales rep onboarding

A confident recommendation starts before you choose a panel or battery.

**Your goal:** collect the essentials and understand what the energy figures mean.

## Ask for the essentials

Use your team’s approved request process to collect:

- The customer’s goal: lower bills, a battery, backup needs or a different priority.
- The installation address and existing solar or battery details.
- A recent electricity bill and the site photos your team requires.
- Roof, switchboard, phase, access or installation details that still need checking.

Read [Collect site information](/quoting/site-information) for the full checklist. Never promise technical suitability from an incomplete form.

## Where the bill and photos come from

<Visibility for="humans">
The bill and photos you review in this lesson are not collected here. They arrive through the **Site Information Form** you sent the customer in [lesson 3](/sales-onboarding/conversation).

That form gives the customer a secure link. They upload their **electricity bill**, **switchboard photos** and a short **cable-run video** through it, and those files then appear on the customer record and on the quote.

If the customer has not completed the form yet, there is nothing real to assess. Open the customer's **Site Information Form** tab and check the badge and the uploads before you continue:

- **Awaiting customer** — the link was sent, but no files have arrived. Wait, or remind the customer.
- **Not requested** — you have not sent the form. Send it before assessing anything.
- **Link expired** — the customer can no longer open the old link. Re-send it.
- **Submitted** — the files are in. Check that the bill belongs to this customer and site and covers the period you need.

Do not estimate usage or rates from memory, from an old bill, or from a home you have not seen.
</Visibility>

<Visibility for="agents">
- **Precondition for the assessment step below:** a real uploaded bill, collected by the **Site Information Form** in [lesson 3](/sales-onboarding/conversation). Nothing is fetched automatically and no lesson creates the bill.
- Read the request status before assessing: `Not requested` (`not_sent`), `Awaiting customer` (`pending`), `Submitted` (`submitted`), `Link expired` (`expired`). Only a genuine customer upload gives genuine values.
- With nothing uploaded, the correct action is to send or re-send the form and wait — never to proceed on assumed or sample values as if they were the customer's. See [Collect site information](/quoting/site-information).
- Once the correct bill is uploaded, the assessment itself is [Analyse an electricity bill](/quoting/bill-analysis).
</Visibility>

## Read a bill with ArgonixIntelligence

1. Open the quote’s bill-assessment flow. With a real customer, select the bill they uploaded through the **Site Information Form**; in the course demo, use an approved sample bill.
2. Choose **Assess bill with ArgonixIntelligence**.
3. Open **Review bill assessment**.
4. Compare the extracted billing period, usage, rates and address with the original.
5. Apply only the values you have checked. Confirm the installation site separately.

The detailed [bill-analysis guide](/quoting/bill-analysis) shows what to review. Keep private customer bills in the approved workflow.

## Explain savings simply

Savings are an estimate based on assumptions, not a promise. Check whether usage means electricity bought from the grid or total household use, especially when the home already has solar.

Confirm the bill period, tariffs, existing system and whether it will be kept or replaced. Ask your manager when an input is unclear.

Try saying: “This estimate uses the usage and rates we reviewed. Your result will depend on how you use the system and future energy prices.”

[Understand the savings estimator](/quoting/savings) before explaining a live result. If your role includes roof layouts, also read [Roof design](/quoting/roof-design) with your manager.
