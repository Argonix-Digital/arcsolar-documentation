Give a customer two options, for example solar only and solar with a battery. Each quote keeps its own price, agreement and payment terms.

## Link two quotes

1. [Create and send](/quoting/walkthrough) both quotes to the same customer.
2. Open [**Quotes**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/quotes).
3. Select **⋯** on either quote and choose **Compare with another estimate**.
4. Under **Quote to compare**, pick the other quote. Check its reference, property and total.
5. Select **Link estimates**.

Both customer links now open the comparison. Linking sends no message and charges nothing.

If you see **No other sent quote**, send the second quote first, then come back.

## What the customer sees

The page is titled **Two offers to compare**. It shows **Option A** and **Option B**, each with:

- **Property** and **Retailer**
- **System**
- **Total after rebates**
- **Payment**, including any indicative finance
- **Rebates included** and **Included in this estimate**

The customer opens one option in full to accept and sign it. Once they accept one, the other can no longer be accepted.

## Remove a comparison

Open **Compare with another estimate** again and select **Remove comparison**. Each quote then opens on its own.

A comparison also ends by itself when you publish a [revision](/quoting/revisions) of either quote, or mark either one as lost.

<Accordion title="A quote is not available to compare">
Both quotes must be for the same customer, sent, and linked to a sales opportunity. You can't compare a quote that is accepted, archived, already in a comparison, has an expired link, or is already being signed. The dialog tells you which rule applies.
</Accordion>
