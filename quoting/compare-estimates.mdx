Link two sent offers for the same customer. They can cover different properties, opportunities or selling brands; each keeps its own agreement and payment terms.

## Link the offers

1. Send both quotes and confirm both customer links are valid.
2. In **Quotes**, open either row's menu and choose **Compare with another estimate**.
3. Check the second offer's reference, type, property, retailer, sent date and total.
4. Select it, then choose **Link estimates**.

Both existing links now open the comparison. Linking does not send a message, sign an agreement or charge a deposit.

## What the customer sees

Desktop shows the offers side by side. Mobile uses **Option A** and **Option B**. Each shows the property, retailer, system, total after rebates, deposit, payment summary and included items.

The customer opens an offer in full to accept or sign. Choosing one returns them to its normal portal; the other can no longer be accepted and redirects while its link remains valid. A signed agreement can be selected while its separate deposit is still pending.

## Change the pair

Before acceptance or signature, reopen the comparison action and choose **Remove comparison**. Each offer then opens separately. Revising or marking an offer Lost also ends its active comparison; history stays in the workspace.

<Accordion title={"An offer is unavailable"}>
Both must belong to the same customer, be sent, have valid links and use separate opportunities. Accepted, archived, signed or already-linked offers are unavailable. Send a draft or current revision before trying again.
</Accordion>
