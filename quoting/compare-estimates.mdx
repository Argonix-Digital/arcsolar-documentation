Give a customer two clear options for the same property. Their existing estimate link opens a comparison of the systems, total prices, deposits, payment summaries and included items. They can open either full estimate before choosing.

<Note>
  Quote comparisons work with **ordinary estimates** that have been sent or viewed. Signable agreements keep their existing signature flow.
</Note>

## Link the estimates

<Steps>
<Step title="Send both estimates">
Create and send two ordinary estimates for the same customer, property, opportunity and selling brand. Each estimate needs a current customer link. See [Create and send a quote](/quoting/walkthrough).
</Step>
<Step title="Open the comparison action">
In [**Quotes**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/quotes), open the three-dot menu on either sent estimate. Select **Compare with another estimate**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the second estimate">
Select an eligible estimate from the list and click **Link estimates**. ArcSolar shows its reference, system type, sent date and total so you can confirm the right option.
</Step>
</Steps>

Linking updates both existing customer links. It does not send a new message or accept either estimate. Ask the customer to open either valid link when they are ready to compare.

## What the customer sees

On a desktop, the customer sees both options side by side. On a phone, they switch between **Option A** and **Option B**. Each option shows its system, total after rebates, deposit, payment summary and included items. **Review option A** or **Review option B** opens that full estimate.

Reviewing an option does not accept it. The customer confirms acceptance on the full estimate. Once they accept one, that estimate returns to the normal single-estimate portal. The other option cannot be accepted; its link directs them to the chosen estimate while that link remains valid. Acceptance and a confirmed deposit are separate events.

## Change or remove a comparison

Before acceptance, open **Compare with another estimate** on either quote row and select **Remove comparison**. Both estimates then open separately again. You can link a different eligible pair afterwards.

Revising an estimate or marking it Lost ends its active comparison. Send and check the current offer before linking again. Comparison history remains in the workspace; linking does not delete or silently mark either estimate Lost.

## If an estimate is missing from the list

Check that both offers are sent or viewed ordinary estimates for the same customer, property, opportunity and selling brand. An accepted, archived or already-linked offer is not eligible. Both customer links must still be valid. If the second offer is a draft, send it first. If a revision ended an earlier comparison, return after the current revision has been sent.

For the normal single-estimate experience, see [Understand the customer's quote view](/quoting/customer-view).

## ArgonixIntelligence in this workflow

Use call or customer context to explain the differences, but review both issued offers yourself. Intelligence does not link estimates, choose for the customer or confirm acceptance or payment.
