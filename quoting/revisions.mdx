A revision updates a quote you already sent. The customer keeps the same link. ArcSolar keeps the old version in staff history.

## Publish a revision

1. Open [**Quotes**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/quotes).
2. Select **⋯** on the quote and choose **Edit quote**. The quote builder opens with the sent quote loaded.
3. Make your changes. The selling brand stays the same.
4. On **Review**, select **Preview quote**.
5. Fill in **Reason for change**. Only your team sees it.
6. Leave **Notify the customer by email and SMS** ticked to tell the customer. Untick it to update quietly.
7. Select **Publish revision**.

**Publish revision** stays off until you enter a reason and clear everything under **Before sending**.

## What changes

- The customer's link shows the new version straight away. Ask them to refresh if the page is already open.
- The revised quote gets its own PDF.
- If the quote was in a [comparison](/quoting/compare-estimates), the comparison ends.

If the customer has no mobile number, or automated SMS is off, only the email is sent. ArcSolar tells you no SMS was sent and why.

## See the history

Select **⋯** on the quote and choose **View estimate history**. **Revision history** lists each version, when it was published, and whether the customer was notified.

## When you can't edit

**Edit quote** does not show once a quote is:

- accepted,
- marked as lost, or
- has a deposit that is processing, paid or refunded.

Some changes need a new quote instead:

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **To change upfront or finance payment, duplicate this quote and send it as a new quote.** | Choose **Duplicate estimate**, change the payment option and send. |
| **Accepted quotes are immutable.** | The customer already accepted. Talk to your manager about next steps. |
| **Another user revised this quote. Reload before editing.** | Reload the page to get the latest version. |

## Duplicate a quote

**Duplicate estimate** in the **⋯** menu starts a new quote with the same details. Use it to make a second option or to change the payment type. The new quote gets its own reference and link.
