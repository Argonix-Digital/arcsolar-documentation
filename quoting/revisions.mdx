Find the offer in [**Quotes**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/quotes) and choose **Edit quote** when available.

## Publish a revision

1. Make the changes in the builder.
2. Check the full customer preview, including price and payment schedule.
3. Enter a staff-only **Reason for change**.
4. Review **Notify the customer by email and SMS**.
5. Choose **Publish revision** and confirm the result.

The quote reference and customer link remain attached. Staff history retains the earlier snapshot and PDF; the revised offer has its own saved document. A revision also ends an active comparison.

## If editing is locked

Acceptance, signature evidence or a processing or settled deposit can lock revision. A refund does not automatically unlock it. Use the supported follow-up process rather than issuing a conflicting offer.
