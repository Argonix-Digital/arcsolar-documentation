A quote can include three kinds of support:

| Kind | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Federal STCs** | Worked out for you from the system, postcode and installation date. They reduce the price. |
| **State rebates and promotions** | Only show if your business offers them. The rep confirms eligibility in the quote. |
| **Interest-free loans** | Repayable. They lower what the customer pays you, not the system price. |

## Federal STCs

STCs are added automatically for solar and batteries. They show in the **Price breakdown** and on the preview as **Federal Solar STCs** and **Federal Battery STCs**. Hot water STCs are handled on the **Hot water** step (see below).

The amount depends on:

- the system size and products,
- the **CER rebate zone** on the **Solar** step (set from the postcode), and
- the **Expected installation date** on the **Review** step.

STCs stay estimates until the installation is done. If you change the date, zone or equipment, check the total again.

In the live estimate, select **Jan 2027** to see what the STCs would be if the system is installed from 1 January 2027. This is only a comparison. Your quote does not change, and eligibility is not confirmed.

## How package prices treat STCs

An admin sets this once in [**Retailer Settings → Pricing & fees**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/quoting-settings), under **Package selling price basis**.

| Setting | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **After federal STCs** | Your package price already has STCs taken off. ArcSolar adds the STC value back to show the full price, then takes it off again as its own line. The total stays your package price. |
| **Before incentives** | Your package price is the full price. ArcSolar takes the STC value off it. |

Example: with **After federal STCs**, a $10,000 package with $2,000 of STCs shows $12,000 before STCs and $10,000 after. Don't take the $2,000 off again.

The same tab sets **Commercial STC price per certificate ($)**, **Hot-water STC price per certificate ($)** and **VEEC price per certificate ($)** for Victorian Energy Upgrades.

## State rebates and loans

Admins choose which programs your team can offer on the [**Rebate Settings**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/energy-data) page. See [Rebate settings](/quoting/energy-data).

When a program is on, it appears on the quote's **Review** step under **Incentives & eligibility**:

1. Answer each eligibility question with **Yes — confirmed by rep**, **No — not eligible** or **Not answered**.
2. Fill in any extra fields, such as **Customer's electricity provider (not the battery brand)** or **Requested loan amount ($)**.
3. Check the amount in the live estimate and the preview.

Hot water and air conditioning rebates, such as **Federal hot water STCs**, **Victorian Energy Upgrades** and the **Solar Victoria hot water rebate**, appear on their own service step under **Rebates**. Tick each item listed to include the rebate.

When you select **Confirm & send**, you also confirm the eligibility requirements you ticked. Answer from what the customer told you or showed you. Your answer is not government or lender approval.

If the customer, address, equipment or date changes, answer the questions again.

## Promotions

Your own offers, such as a dollar discount for a month, are set up under **Retailer promotions** on the Rebate Settings page. They are kept separate from government support.
