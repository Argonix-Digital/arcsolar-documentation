A customer moves through sales, delivery and close-out. Keep the next action and its owner clear at every handoff.

## From enquiry to quote

1. **Capture the enquiry.** Find or create the [customer record](/customers/records). Confirm contact details, source, consent and owner.
2. **Have the conversation.** Use [Comms](/customers/calls-sms), record the outcome and set the next [task](/customers/tasks).
3. **Understand the site.** Request the [bill, photos and cable-run video](/quoting/site-information). Review what comes back.
4. **Prepare the offer.** [Create a quote](/quoting/walkthrough), check the customer preview and send it when ready.

## From decision to installation

Follow the quote's viewed, accepted and payment states separately. Coordinate [automatic follow-ups](/quoting/follow-ups) with the rep's conversation.

Open the linked [project](/delivery/projects). Confirm the agreed scope, funding, equipment and installer before setting a date. Use [Calendar](/delivery/calendar) and [Field Operations](/delivery/field-operations) to coordinate the work.

## Close the loop

Collect installation evidence, complete compliance and rebate follow-up, hand over to the customer and [reconcile payments](/delivery/finance).

An accepted quote is not a paid deposit. A paid deposit is not installation readiness. A completed visit is not a closed project.

## Leave a useful handover

Record what was agreed, what is missing, who acts next and when. Put outstanding work in an owned task so the next person can pick it up.
