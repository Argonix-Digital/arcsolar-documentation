---
title: "The customer journey"
description: "Know the handoffs from enquiry to close-out."
---

Use this as the end-to-end operating procedure. Open the linked guide whenever you need the exact steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture and assign the enquiry">
    Search **Customers → Customer Records** before creating a record. Confirm contact details, lead source, owner and communication consent. [Meta leads](/integrations/meta) can arrive automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Qualify and record the next action">
    Call or message from the customer record or **Comms**. Record the outcome, update the appropriate stage and create an owned follow-up task. Gather the address, electricity bill, goals and site information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assess and quote">
    Open **Quick Create Quote**. Check the customer, equipment, installation conditions, [bill analysis](/quoting/bill-analysis), [savings](/quoting/savings), incentives, payment schedule and internal BOM. Preview before sending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up and confirm the outcome">
    Check the sent/viewed/accepted state and the customer's scheduled messages. Keep manual outreach coordinated with [automatic journeys](/quoting/follow-ups). Check acceptance and payment separately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the installation">
    Open the linked project. Confirm customer information, funding, installer, equipment and readiness. Set a date only when the required prerequisites are satisfied.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver and close out">
    Track the job, collect completion evidence, resolve compliance and rebate work, and reconcile outstanding payments. Complete the remaining tasks before close-out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep these records distinct

| Record | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Who are we working with? |
| Opportunity or deal | What are they considering buying? |
| Quote | What offer and terms did we issue? |
| Task | Who must do what, and by when? |
| Project and job | What are we delivering, and what is its progress? |
| Payment record | What is owed, processing or paid? |

Accepted does not mean paid. Paid does not mean ready for installation. Installed does not mean compliance and financial close-out are finished.

## ArgonixIntelligence in this workflow

Use dashboard briefs to spot the next priority, call summaries to prepare the conversation and bill analysis to prepare the quote. Confirm the underlying record before taking action.
