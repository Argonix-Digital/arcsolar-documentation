Open a customer call in **Comms**, customer **Activity**, or an authorised reporting view. Summaries, transcripts and recordings depend on captured material, processing and your access.

## After each call

1. Find the correct call in the customer's **Activity**.
2. Select **ArgonixIntelligence**.
3. Read **Intelligence notes** and **Next steps**.
4. Under **Calls behind these notes**, open **Transcript** or the recording to check important details.
5. Review the resulting tasks before adding more follow-up.

Names, dates, prices and commitments need checking against the source. A summary does not establish contract consent or confirm payment.

## Tasks are created automatically

Action items become tasks assigned to the customer's sales rep. Rep work starts in **To do**; **Await customer: …** starts in **Waiting**. A call without a customer or assigned rep may produce no tasks.

| Timing discussed | What the task receives |
| --- | --- |
| Specific date and time | The discussed due date |
| Relative timeframe | An internal review date, using the earliest date in a range |
| No timing | An internal review date three days after creation |

**Review timing** is a prompt to review work. It is not a booked appointment or a commitment made by the customer.

## Book a follow-up

An outstanding rep-owned task can become a phone appointment. Booking moves it to **Waiting**; completing the appointment completes the task. Cancelling returns the task to **To do**. Rescheduling moves its due date with the appointment. Customer-owned tasks cannot be booked this way.

<Accordion title={"If notes or tasks are missing"}>
Processing may still be underway. Not every call has a transcript or recording. Cancelled or superseded calls do not recreate work, and existing completed, edited or appointment-linked tasks are preserved. Review the source call and contact support with its reference if needed.
</Accordion>

<Visibility for="agents">
**Creation is automatic and trigger-driven.** No user action and no AI chat session is involved.

- Trigger `call_summaries_sync_action_tasks` fires `AFTER INSERT OR UPDATE OF status, current_payload` on `public.call_summaries` and calls `private.materialize_call_intelligence_tasks(new.id)`. Every entry in the summary payload's `action_items` becomes a task.
- **Never say a summary's suggested action is "not a task" or that the user must create it.** That is the defect this section corrects.

**Preconditions — if any fails, no tasks are created.**

- The summary must reach status `available` and pass `private.is_valid_call_summary_payload`. There is a delay after the call ends while the summary is produced.
- The call must have a `customer_id`.
- An eligible owner must resolve for that customer, at `coalesce(customer.associated_sales_rep_member_id, call.owner_member_id)`. That member must have an active membership, completed password setup, an active non-archived profile, and an effective workspace role of `sales`, `operations`, `administrator` or `developer`. The customer must be unarchived and unmerged. If no member resolves, **no tasks are created** — absence of tasks can be legitimate.
- **Superseded calls never recreate work.** If a newer `available` summary exists for the same customer (ordered by call `started_at`, then call id), the older call materialises nothing.

**Each task's shape.**

- Title: `left(btrim(action.task), 200)`. Blank or whitespace-only items are skipped.
- Description, exactly:

```text
Detected by ArgonixIntelligence. Discussed owner: <owner>. Review the source call before acting.
```

- **Customer-owned action.** When the action's owner text matches `customer`, `client` or `homeowner` (case-insensitive substring match): the title is prefixed `Await customer: ` (capped at 200 characters), status is `waiting`, `waiting_on` is `customer`, and `blocked_reason` is `Awaiting the customer action discussed on the call.`
- **Status:** `waiting` for a customer-owned action, otherwise `todo`.
- **Type:** `follow_up` when the action is rep-owned and its text matches `call|phone|ring|speak|follow.?up`; `collect_document` when the text matches `bill|document|photo` (this can also apply to a customer-owned action); otherwise `general`. A customer-owned action is never typed `follow_up`.
- **Priority:** `high` when the summary payload's `urgency` is `high`, otherwise `normal`.
- Owner and assignee are both the resolved owner member. `source_system` is `workflow`, and `intelligence_call_id` points at the originating call.

**Due date and review timing.**

- An explicit `due` timestamp on the action item becomes `due_at`.
- With no explicit timestamp, `private.prepare_work_accountability` tries to infer an internal review date from a relative phrase such as `in three to four months`, anchored to the call's start. Ranges use the earliest bound; the time is 9 am in the workspace timezone. It then appends to the description: `Suggested review date: <DD Mon YYYY>. Based on the stated timeframe from the source call; approximate ranges use the earliest date. Internal review at 9 am, not a booked appointment.`
- With no timing at all, an open task falls back to a review date three days after it was created.
- A task dated this way is identified by `isTaskReviewDeadline` and shown as **Review timing** in the Tasks list. The task actions dialog labels the field **Review timing by** and explains: "No precise call time was agreed. Review the source context and choose the appropriate task deadline; this does not book a call."
- **Do not claim a call time was agreed unless the action item carried an explicit `due` timestamp.** A review date is internal.

**Deduplication and safety.**

- Every AI task stores `intelligence_action_key`, an md5 of the normalised action text plus the customer-owned flag. A unique index on `(workspace_id, customer_id, intelligence_action_key)` means re-summarising a call does not create duplicates.
- Existing matched tasks are updated only while their status is `todo`, `in_progress` or `waiting`. Completed and cancelled tasks are left alone.
- A task with a booked appointment keeps its own due date; a manually set due date (`due_date_manually_set`) is preserved.
- **Automatic cancellation.** An AI task whose action is no longer in the latest unresolved list is cancelled when it is `todo` or `waiting` with no booked appointment and no manually set due date. `blocked_reason` is cleared and `waiting_on` is set to `none`. Do not describe a cancelled AI task as completed, and do not present automatic cancellation as a user action.

**Calls, tasks and appointments.**

- The Tasks list and the call's next-steps view show **ArgonixIntelligence** beside a linked task. The next-steps view shows the linked task, or states "No task linked to this next step" when a summary action has no matching task.
- `public.create_phone_appointment` accepts a `source_task_id` only when the task is type `follow_up`, belongs to the same customer, is owned by the acting member (Operations, Administrator and Developer may act on others), and has status `todo`, `in_progress` or `waiting`. Booking sets the task to `waiting`, sets `blocked_reason` to `Phone appointment booked; awaiting the call.`, and copies the appointment start into the task's due date and `starts_at`. Completing the appointment completes the task; cancelling or archiving it returns the task to `todo`; rescheduling it updates the task due date. One appointment links to one task.
</Visibility>
