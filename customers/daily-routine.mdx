Follow this routine every day. It keeps leads moving and stops anyone slipping through.

## Start the day

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your Rep Overview">
    Open [**Rep Overview**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/overview). Look at **Appointments**, **Follow-up due** and **Work Requiring Attention**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear your Inbox">
    Open the [Inbox](/customers/inbox). Deal with missed calls, customer uploads and mentions first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick up new leads">
    In the [Pipeline](/customers/pipeline), check the **New** column. Claim or ask for any unassigned leads, then make first contact quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work your tasks">
    Open [Tasks](/customers/tasks), choose **Overdue**, then **Today**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## For each customer

1. Open the customer record. Read the latest messages, calls and notes.
2. Call or message them from **Actions → Communicate**.
3. After a call, save the **Review lead disposition** form with notes and a follow-up reminder. See [Calls and SMS](/customers/calls-sms#after-the-call).
4. Move the lead in the [Pipeline](/customers/pipeline) when it has really progressed.
5. Make sure there is a next task with an owner and a due date.

After a call, check the tasks [ArgonixIntelligence](/customers/call-intelligence) created before adding your own. Check [scheduled follow-ups](/quoting/follow-ups) so automatic messages and your outreach don't clash.

## End the day

Check tomorrow's appointments and any open tasks. Complete finished tasks with a short **Completion note**. A good note says what the customer wants, what was agreed and what happens next:

> Comparing two battery options. Waiting on switchboard photos. Sam to call Thursday after 3 pm.
