**Open:** [**Reporting → Lead management**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/reporting?reportTab=lead-management).

| Role | What the report covers |
| --- | --- |
| Administrator, Operations, Finance | All leads |
| Sales representative | Leads assigned to you |
| External marketing | The customer records shared with you. This is the only report you see |

## Choose the period

Use **Reporting period** at the top right: **Last 30 days**, **Last quarter** or **Last 12 months**. Leads are grouped by the date they were received.

## What it shows

**Summary:** **Leads received**, **Contacted**, **Qualified** and **Converted**.

**Disposition breakdown:**

- **Lead dispositions** shows where each lead stands now. Each lead counts once.
- **Call outcomes** shows the result of each call made to those leads in the period.
- Sort by **Most common** or **Alphabetical**, show **Count** or **Percentage**, and switch between chart and table.

**Source quality & follow-up:** one row per **Lead sellers** (lead seller or sub-source) or per **Sales reps**. Columns are **Received**, **Contacted**, **Qualified**, **Converted**, **Conversion**, **No calls in period** and **Calls**. Select a column heading to sort. Use the search box to find a name.

## Read it fairly

- **Contacted** means a first response was recorded: a call, SMS or a manual update.
- A missed call does not mean the lead was poor.
- Recent leads and small numbers need more time before you judge them.
- A lead stays credited to the person or Meta connection that brought it in.

External marketing users also see an **All accounts** button. It shows lead delivery, sales outcomes and Meta connection status across every business that shares records with you.
