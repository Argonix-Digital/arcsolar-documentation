**Rep Overview** is a personal work queue: appointments, open pipeline, follow-up work, delivery waiting on others and balances needing attention.

**Sales** focuses on sales activity and offer outcomes. Use its period and owner filters to understand the displayed pipeline and performance.

**Reporting** brings together management views. Open the relevant tab:

| Tab | What it summarises |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Lifecycle volume, conversion path and exceptions requiring attention |
| Sales & Pipeline | Customer engagement, lead-source performance and rep phone activity |
| Accountability Report | Task ownership, overdue work, escalation and completion |
| Operations | Delivery performance and operational progress |
| Platform information | Platform/billing information and setup context available to your role |

## Read reports consistently

Check the period, timezone, owner filter, last update and whether the screen uses live or mock data. Phone reporting can have its own period selector. A count from a different time window is not directly comparable.

Open the underlying customer, call, task or project when investigating a metric. Treat empty/unavailable data differently from a confirmed zero.

## Recommended review rhythm

Review urgent exceptions daily. Review sales activity and unresolved delivery work weekly. Reconcile finance and the period-specific company reports at month end.

## ArgonixIntelligence in this workflow

The reporting brief changes with the selected tab where enabled. Use its suggested next action, then verify the contributing records.
