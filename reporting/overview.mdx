ArcSolar has three places to check numbers:

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| [**Rep Overview**](/reporting/rep-overview) | Your day: appointments, follow-ups, open quotes and tasks |
| [**Sales**](/reporting/sales) | One rep's quotes, conversion, deal goal and recent wins |
| [**Reporting**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/reporting) | Team and business reports, in tabs |

## What you see depends on your role

| Role | Reporting shows |
| --- | --- |
| Administrator, Operations | Every tab |
| Finance | Every tab except **Accountability Report** |
| Sales manager | **Team reporting**: you and the reps approved for you |
| Sales representative | **Phone performance** and **Lead management** for your own work |
| External marketing | **Lead management** for the customer records shared with you |
| Technician | No reporting |

Sales managers and External marketing users who open **Rep Overview** are sent straight to their report.

## Reporting tabs

Open [**Reporting**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/reporting) and pick a tab.

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Four headline numbers, **Lifecycle Volume**, **Conversion Path** and **Work Requiring Attention** |
| **Sales reporting** | Phone time and selling stats per team member. See [Sales reporting and pipeline](/reporting/sales-pipeline) |
| **Lead management** | Lead quality and follow-up. See [Lead management](/reporting/lead-management) |
| **Pipeline** | **Customer engagement cadence** and **Lead Source Performance**. See [Sales reporting and pipeline](/reporting/sales-pipeline#pipeline-tab) |
| **Deal Outcomes** | Why deals were won, lost or disqualified. Only appears if it is turned on for your business |
| **Accountability Report** | Open, overdue and completed tasks per person. See [Accountability](/reporting/accountability) |
| **Operations** | Scheduled, completed and rescheduled work by type. See [Operations and platform](/reporting/operations-platform) |
| **Platform information** | Monthly call minutes, SMS and platform fees. See [Operations and platform](/reporting/operations-platform#platform-information) |

The four headline numbers at the top are **Lead-to-deal conversion**, **First response**, **Quote acceptance** and **Completed installs**. Each one compares with the period before.

## Filter a report

- **Reporting period:** choose **Last 30 days**, **Last quarter** or **Last 12 months** at the top right.
- **Sales reporting** has its own phone period (from **Today** to **All time**).
- **Selling brand:** if your business has more than one selling brand, choose **All selling brands**, one brand or **Unattributed**. Phone activity, engagement, accountability and platform costs always cover the whole business.

The period and tab are saved in the page address, so you can bookmark or share a view.

## Team reporting (Sales managers)

Sales managers see **Team reporting** instead of the tabs. It lists you and your approved reps with **New customers**, **Open opportunities**, **Quotes sent**, **Accepted sales** and **Calls**. Choose the period at the top right.

If only your own row shows, ask an Administrator to add reps to your profile in [Team & Access](/setup/team).

## Communications archive

Administrators see a **Communications archive** button on **Reporting**. It holds read-only SMS history kept from phone numbers that changed owner. Opening a thread is recorded.

## Exporting

Reports have no export button. To take customer data out, use **Export CSV** in the **Customers** actions menu, if your role allows it.

## Read numbers with care

- Check the period before you compare two numbers.
- **Mock data** means sample numbers, not your business.
- Open the customer, call, task or project behind a number to see the detail.
- A blank or unavailable value is not the same as zero.
