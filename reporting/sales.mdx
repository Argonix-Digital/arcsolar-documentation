**Open:** [**Sales**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/sales) in the sidebar.

**Reporting for** shows whose numbers you are looking at. Administrators can choose another team member. Everyone else sees their own numbers.

## What it shows

| Area | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Quotes sent this month** | Total value of estimates sent, compared with last month |
| **Estimate Acceptance Rate** | Accepted estimates as a share of estimates sent |
| **Open estimates** | Estimates sent this month with no won or lost outcome |
| **Lead-to-Deal Rate** | Assigned leads that became closed deals |
| **Qualified Lead Flow** | Leads assigned and estimates sent over the chosen period |
| **Appointments** | Upcoming bookings, with **Book appointment** |
| **Monthly Deal Goal** | Deals won this month against the target |
| **Recently won deals** | The latest accepted quotes, with deal size and recorded commission |

If your business has more than one selling brand, use **Selling brand** to filter. Appointments and the deal goal always cover all brands.

## Check a result

1. Choose the rep in **Reporting for** (Administrators only).
2. Open a quote in **Recently won deals** to check its customer, deposit and finance steps.
3. Make changes in **Customers** or **Quotes**, not here. This page only reports.

Accepted quote value is not cash collected. For payments, use [Finance](/delivery/finance).

## External marketing

External marketing users see a different **Sales** page with **Sales follow-up** and **Rep analysis** for the leads they supplied.

For call minutes and per-rep phone reports, open [Sales reporting](/reporting/sales-pipeline).
