**Open:** [**Rep Overview**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/overview) in the sidebar.

Rep Overview shows one person's work. **Reporting for** at the top names that person. Administrators can switch to another team member there. Everyone else sees their own work.

Sales managers go to **Team reporting** instead, and External marketing users go to **Lead management**. See [Dashboard overview](/reporting/overview).

## What each card shows

| Card | What it shows | Opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Appointments** | Upcoming bookings. **Book appointment** adds one | **Calendar** |
| **Open pipeline** | Value and count of sent estimates with no won or lost outcome | **Open Sales** |
| **Follow-up due** | Customers with follow-up tasks for this person, and how many are overdue | **Open Customers** |
| **Awaiting operations** | Won deals not yet installed, waiting on other teams | **Open Jobs** |
| **Outstanding balances** | Payments now due on jobs where a deposit was paid | **Open Finance** |
| **Qualified Lead Flow** | Leads assigned and estimates sent over the period | |
| **Top performer this week** | Most accepted quotes since Monday | |
| **Estimate engagement** | Whether customers opened recent estimates, and how often | |
| **Work Requiring Attention** | Tasks overdue, due within seven days or in progress | **Open Tasks** |
| **Recent Estimates Sent** | The latest estimates with system, price and status | |

## Use it each morning

1. Check **Appointments** for today.
2. Open **Work Requiring Attention** and start with overdue tasks.
3. Check **Follow-up due** and **Estimate engagement** to choose who to call.
4. Open the customer or task to do the work. The cards update from those records.

A blank or unavailable count is not the same as zero.
