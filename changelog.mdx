<Update label="Version 2.0.0" tags={["Major"]} description="21 October 2026" rss={{ title: "ArcSolar v2 is here", description: "A cleaner workspace, simpler navigation and clearer tools for every stage of the customer journey.", date: "2026-10-21" }}>
  ## ArcSolar v2 is here

  ArcSolar v2 brings a cleaner, more focused workspace to your everyday work. From the first enquiry to installation, the next action is easier to find and the details are there when you need them.

  <Frame caption="The ArcSolar v2 customer register, shown with fictional demo records.">
    <img src="/images/v2/customer-records.png" alt="ArcSolar v2 Customer Records with a compact customer register, search and display controls." />
  </Frame>

  ### What’s new

  - **A clearer workspace.** Compact customer lists, pipeline boards and project views put the customer and next action first.
  - **More direct navigation.** Search the workspace, move between pages with keyboard shortcuts and choose your own start page.
  - **Files and financials together.** Find project documents and review balances, deposits and payment history from the project.
  - **Portals made for the job.** Customers can review their quote, follow installation progress and provide site information. Technicians get a focused view of their assigned work and files.
  - **A lighter introduction.** A short welcome covers appearance, search and optional team and rebate setup. Required business configuration stays available through the setup checklist.
  - **Appearance that follows you.** Choose your interface theme and contrast in account preferences.

  [Open ArcSolar](https://app.arcsolar.com.au) or explore the [fictional demo](https://demo.arcsolar.com.au/demo).

  For a guided start, see the [workspace guide](/reference/workspace), [project workflow](/delivery/projects) and [customer portal guide](/quoting/customer-view).
</Update>
