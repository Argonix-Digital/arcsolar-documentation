## More ways to plan your team's week

The operations calendar adds Week, Month, Day, Agenda and time-blocking views, member and role filters, fullscreen browsing and event details. You can add bounded weekly blocks, link customers and contacts, and subscribe to a revocable, read-only personal or team calendar.

Retailer Settings also keeps its brand controls within the available content width on smaller screens.

[Use the operations calendar](/delivery/calendar).

</Update>

Retailer Settings now groups its controls into five main sections. ArcSolar Settings and Profile Settings each use four main sections. Advanced options and retained approvals open when needed, and settings search takes you to the relevant section.
