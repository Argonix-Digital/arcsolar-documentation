## Your path

2. [Meet your customer](/sales-onboarding/customer)
3. [Make the conversation count](/sales-onboarding/conversation)

Your manager handles billing, integrations, permissions and pricing policy. During training, stop before contacting real customers, sending an offer or collecting payment.

If you get stuck, ask your manager about access or commercial decisions. For a software issue, [contact support](/reference/support).
