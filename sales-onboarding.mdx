## Before you start

Your manager invites you and sets up your access. If you're the manager, follow [Invite your team](/setup/team):

1. Invite the rep with the **Sales** role.
2. Choose **Yes** for **Does this user need a phone number?** if they will call customers.
3. Tick the selling brands they may sell under.
4. Give them a practice customer to learn on.

During training, use only the practice customer. Don't call or text real customers, send a quote or take a payment until your manager says you're ready.

Stuck on access or pricing? Ask your manager. Something not working? [Contact support](/reference/support).
