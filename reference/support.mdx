## Report a problem from inside ArcSolar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Select **Report a problem** at the bottom of the sidebar. It is also on the **Comms** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what happened">
    In **What went wrong?**, say what you selected, what you expected and what actually happened. Use at least 10 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a screenshot (optional)">
    Select **Attach a screenshot**, or paste an image from your clipboard. PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 2 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Select **Send report**. You see **Problem reported** when it has gone through.
  </Step>
</Steps>

ArcSolar adds the page you were on, your browser and device, the app version and a short screen replay with all text and typing hidden. Don't include passwords or customer financial details.

If the screenshot fails to upload, your written report is still sent.

## Email support

Email **[support@arcsolar.com.au](mailto:support@arcsolar.com.au)** when you can't sign in, the app won't load, or the problem affects your whole team.

Include:

- Your company name and the page you were on.
- What you expected and what happened.
- The customer, quote, project or task you were working on.
- Roughly when it happened.
- Any error message you saw.
- Whether it affects one person or everyone.
- A screenshot, with private details hidden.

Never email passwords, access keys, full bank details or private customer links. Support will arrange a safe way to look at sensitive details if needed.

## Before you report

| Problem | Check first |
| --- | --- |
| A page or button is missing | Your role. See [Feature availability](/reference/availability). |
| A connected service isn't updating | The connection in Settings and when it last updated. See [Connect your tools](/integrations/overview). |
| A payment looks wrong | Whether it shows as processing, failed or paid. |
| Calls don't work | Microphone permission in your browser, and that a phone number is assigned to you. |

## Spotted something out of date in these docs?

Send the page link and a screenshot of what you see in the app to **[support@arcsolar.com.au](mailto:support@arcsolar.com.au)**.

<Visibility for="agents">
- **Report a problem** is the main in-app route for software problems. It sits in the sidebar footer and on the Comms page, and is rendered only in a **live** workspace (not in the fictional demo). Do not claim ArcSolar has no in-app reporting.
- Dialog: **Report a problem**. Required **What went wrong?** (at least 10 characters, at most 4,000). Optional **Screenshot** via **Attach a screenshot** or paste: PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 2 MB. Actions **Cancel** and **Send report**.
- Validation: under 10 characters, "Add a little more detail so we can investigate."; wrong type, "Attach a PNG, JPEG, or WebP image."; too large, "The screenshot must be 2 MB or smaller."
- Success toast **Problem reported**. If only the screenshot fails, the toast is "Report sent without the screenshot" and the text report is still delivered. A send failure asks the user to wait and try again.
- Diagnostics attached automatically: masked session replay, page, browser and device, app version and recent error codes. The report becomes a tracked item for the ArcSolar team. It is not a feature-request channel; feedback goes through **Send feedback** (Profile → Help & feedback), which opens an email.
- **ArcSolar status** in the account menu links to arcsolar.openstatus.dev.
</Visibility>
