## Using ArcSolar

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Workspace | Your company's account in ArcSolar. If you work for more than one company, you can switch between workspaces. |
| Role | What you do in the company, such as Sales representative or Operations. Your role decides which pages you see. |
| Inbox | Your list of things that need you: tasks, mentions, missed calls and customer uploads |
| Quick search | The search box you open with **⌘K** or **Ctrl K** to jump to any page, setting or help guide |
| Task | A follow-up with an owner and a due date |
| SOPs | Your company's own written processes, found in Settings |

## Sales

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Lead | A new enquiry |
| Opportunity | A possible sale that a rep is working on |
| Pipeline | All open opportunities, grouped by stage |
| Quote / estimate | An offer sent to a customer, with its price and terms saved |
| Revision | A new version of a quote that has already been sent |
| Selling brand | The business name a customer buys from, with its own logo, contract and prices. Not the same as a product brand, such as a panel maker. |
| Original brand | Your company's main selling brand, marked **(default brand)** |
| Additional brand | Another selling brand added later. It can save quote drafts but can't send quotes yet. |
| Price book | The set of prices a selling brand quotes from |

## Solar and energy

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| kW | Power, or the size of a system |
| kWh | Energy used, made or stored |
| Grid imports | Electricity bought from the grid |
| Feed-in tariff | What the customer is paid for electricity sent to the grid |
| NMI | National Metering Identifier: the ID of an electricity connection |
| BOM | Bill of materials: the equipment and quantities for a job |
| Roof Vision | The roof design tool. You open it from a quote or from **Roof Vision** in the sidebar. It is part of the **Arc** plan. |
| Panel layout | A drawing of where the panels sit on the roof |
| Stringing | How panels are wired together into groups (strings), drawn in Roof Vision |
| MPPT | A separate input on an inverter. Each string connects to one. |
| Single line diagram (SLD) | The electrical drawing of the system, made in Roof Vision |

## Rebates and payments

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| STC | Small-scale technology certificate: the basis of the federal solar rebate |
| Rebate | Money off under a government program, subject to its rules |
| Government loan | Money the customer pays back. It is not a discount. |
| Deposit | The first payment in the customer's payment schedule |
| PayTo | A way for a customer to approve payments straight from their bank account |
| Stripe | The service that collects card and bank payments from your customers |

## Calls and messages

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Comms | ArcSolar's calls and SMS page |
| SMS segment | One billed part of a text message. Long messages use more than one. |
| Call intelligence | Call summaries and transcripts, where your company uses them |
