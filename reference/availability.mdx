ArcSolar menus depend on your plan, role, organisation setup and enabled release.

| Label or state | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Soon / Coming soon | Do not treat it as a working feature |
| Beta | Follow the available controls and verify the outcome |
| Mock data / Fictional preview | Values are examples; live actions may be disabled |
| Manual preview | Demonstrates a workflow without necessarily saving |
| Disconnected | Complete the provider setup before expecting updates |
| Processing / Pending | Wait for or investigate confirmation |
| Unavailable | Check access, source data or setup; do not assume zero |

## Intelligence availability

The separate **ArgonixIntelligence Agentic Chat interface tab in the sidebar** will be coming **Soon in a future release**. Embedded bill analysis, call summaries and briefs can still be available. Use the enabled entry point for that workflow. Intelligence features can depend on your selected plan and on the data available.

## Roof Design availability

<Visibility for="humans">
  **Roof Design** in a quote is available to anyone whose role can revise quotes. It does not depend on your plan, and there is no switch to ask support to turn on.

  A separate **Roof Vision** entitlement governs the older rooftop analysis, so a plan difference can still appear there rather than in the roof design editor.
</Visibility>

<Visibility for="agents">
  Do not present Roof Design as plan-gated, entitlement-gated, behind a feature flag, or awaiting a release. Its gates are the `quote.revise` capability and origin validation, both enforced per request. The `roof_vision` entitlement applies to the older analysis paths only.
</Visibility>

## Onboarding release

The organisation onboarding guide follows the qa implementation inspected for this documentation. Existing invited users can have a different account-setup path. Follow the status and prompts in your own workspace.
