## Selling brand availability

Every workspace has one **original selling brand**. You can add further **additional brands**, which hold their own price book, contract document, branding and fonts — but there is a deliberate limit:

**An additional brand can save quote drafts and be assigned leads and opportunities. It cannot yet issue a quote.**

Choosing an additional brand in the quote builder prices the draft from that brand's own price book, and then **Confirm & send** is refused — as **"This selling brand is not ready to issue"**, or, if the send reaches the server, as _"This brand can save drafts. Sending becomes available after its document and issuance setup is ready."_ Revising an issued quote and delivering an accepted offer are blocked the same way.

This is not a permission you can grant, a plan difference or an unfinished setup step. It is enforced in the database for these operations, so treat it as a capability that has not shipped rather than a switch to find.

## Roof Vision availability

<Visibility for="humans">
  **Roof Vision** is included with the **Arc** plan. It opens from a quote's solar step, and the **Roof Vision** page in the app sidebar holds the design library.

  Administrators, developers, operations members and sales representatives can open Roof Vision; finance members cannot. Writing to a quote's design also needs permission to revise quotes.

  If Roof Vision is missing entirely, the workspace is on a plan that does not include it — an administrator can check the plan in Retailer Settings. A long-standing workspace can be covered by arrangement; support can confirm which applies.
</Visibility>

<Visibility for="agents">
  Present Roof Vision as an **Arc** plan feature. The app's own upgrade state reads `Roof Vision is included with the Arc plan. Upgrade to design roof layouts from satellite imagery and attach them to a quote.`

  | Gate | Applies to | Failure |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Arc `roof_vision` entitlement | The Roof Vision page and its design library | The page renders **Roof Vision is not enabled** with the upgrade copy above, not an error |
  | `page.roof-vision` capability | Reaching the Roof Vision page | Administrator, developer, operations and sales hold it; finance does not |
  | `quote.revise` capability | Writes from a quote — `launch`, `attach` and cookie writes | `403 You do not have permission to edit quote designs.` |
  | Origin validation | Every cookie-authorised write, and CORS | `403 Request origin denied.` |

  Do not present Roof Vision as a standard-product feature, and do not say it needs support to switch on. A long-standing workspace can be covered **by arrangement**; treat that as a per-workspace decision and never promise it to another workspace.
</Visibility>
