This page sums up the biggest changes since ArcSolar v2. For every release, read the [changelog](/changelog).

In the app, open your account menu (your name at the bottom of the sidebar). The **What's new** list shows the latest updates. Select one to read it here.

## November 2026

**Activity you can scan (13 November).** Customer and project activity shows who did what and when. Select a row to open what it describes, such as a payment or an accepted quote.

**Grid connections (12–13 November).** Every project has a **Grid Connection** tab. ArcSolar works out the network from the NMI and lines up the values and files the network's portal asks for. Customers can now have more than one property, each with its own NMI, retailer and quotes.

**Sales managers (4 November).** A new **Sales manager** role works their own customers and those of the sales reps you approve for them, with team reporting. See [Set up a sales manager](/setup/team#set-up-a-sales-manager).

## October 2026

**Agent (29 October).** Ask ArcSolar about your customers and jobs, get reports, and review actions before they run. It is in beta. See [Agent](/agent).

**More than solar (22 October).** Quote air conditioning, hot water heat pumps and EV chargers, on their own or with solar and battery. See [Create a quote](/quoting/walkthrough).

**ArcSolar v2 (21 October).** A cleaner layout, quick search, keyboard shortcuts and a start page you choose. See [Find your way around](/reference/workspace).
