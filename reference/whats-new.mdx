## Major updates published on 5 October 2026

The latest major releases cover scheduling and calendar foundations, plus the job portal and technician sign-in. Read the [full changelog](/changelog) for the feature details and release history.

### Calendar, availability and booking safeguards

Use the calendar's month, week, day and list views, fullscreen timetable, event search, team/role filters and colors by event type or team member. Keep appointments, meetings, time blocks and delivery work linked to their records.

Sales reps configure separate onsite and phone/video hours, confirm them on first sign-in and every 28 days, and edit them from Calendar or Profile Settings. Busy blocks protect holidays and one-off commitments. Incoming subscribed calendars import private, read-only busy time.

Managers approve service areas, channel eligibility and buffers. New policies default to 45-minute phone/video consultations with a 10-minute gap, or 60-minute in-person consultations with a 30-minute gap. Existing saved settings remain unchanged. Booking checks enforce availability, conflicts and travel requirements before assigning an unassigned customer.

Once a manager enables appointment messages, eligible customers and reps receive SMS/email confirmations and reminders 24 hours and 30 minutes beforehand. Rescheduling and cancellation update the reminder queue. Contacts, consent, preferences and sender readiness still apply.

Read [Calendar and scheduling](/delivery/calendar) and [Sales availability setup](/setup/sales-availability).

### Job portal and technician sign-in

Invited Technician users can sign in by email link and open their named, assigned field visits when the retailer's Work capability is enabled. The office opens the linked portal from Projects & Jobs to review the shared job information, visit history, forms and evidence.

A copied portal link does not grant access. The technician needs their own active account and current assignment. A site visit's completion remains separate from project close-out, compliance, rebates and finance.

Read [Job portal and technician access](/delivery/job-portal) and [Field operations](/delivery/field-operations).

## Before you enable automatic bookings

- Ask reps to save and confirm their actual hours, timezone and notification destinations.
- Review service-area postcodes, channel policies, minimum buffers and calendar coverage in **Calendar → Booking settings**.
- Check required subscribed-feed health and authorized SMS/email sender readiness.
- Enable eligible reps and workspace appointment delivery through the manager controls.

The staff **Scheduling tools** harness prepares a booking, reschedule or cancellation for review and explicit confirmation. It does not enable the AI voice appointment setter. Native technician Work access is also a separate rollout.

## How in-app updates appear

Highlights tagged **major** or **feature** appear in **New in ArcSolar** in the signed-in workspace. Previously dismissed releases stay dismissed; a new release has its own identity. An existing welcome announcement may appear before the newer release highlights. The fictional demo does not show automatic member announcements.

These highlights use the published changelog feed. They are release announcements, rather than a timer that automatically sends a weekly notification. You can open [Changelog](/changelog) whenever you need the full history.

## ArgonixIntelligence in this workflow

Intelligence can explain available workflows and current readiness. It cannot grant a role, complete a rep's confirmation, bypass a booking conflict, manufacture delivery proof or replace technician compliance and safety checks. Ask [support](/reference/support) if an expected feature or access is missing.
