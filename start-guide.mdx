ArcSolar connects customer conversations, quotes and installation work. Start with a customer record and follow the work from there.

## Find your bearings

Sign in to your workspace. **Rep Overview** shows what needs attention; **Customers** holds your customer records; **Quotes** holds your offers.

Use app search with **⌘ J** on Mac or **Ctrl J** on Windows. [Workspace basics](/reference/workspace) covers navigation and personal settings.

## Work with a customer

1. Open **Customers → Customer Records** and search before adding someone new.
2. Check their contact details, installation address and assigned rep.
3. Read **Activity** before making a call or sending a message.
4. Leave a clear next action in **Tasks**, with an owner and a date.

[Customer records](/customers/records), [calls and SMS](/customers/calls-sms), and [tasks](/customers/tasks) cover the details.

## Prepare your first quote

Collect the [site information](/quoting/site-information) and electricity bill. Open **Quick Create Quote**, choose the customer, review their energy use and select the system. Check the total, payment schedule and customer preview before sending.

Use a manager-approved practice record while learning. Stop at the preview; sending a quote or contacting a real customer creates real work.
