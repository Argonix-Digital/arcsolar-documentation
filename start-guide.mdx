ArcSolar keeps your customers, conversations, quotes and installations in one place. This guide gets you through your first day.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open [app.arcsolar.com.au](https://app.arcsolar.com.au) and sign in with the email your company invited. Most people land on **Inbox**, the list of things that need you.

    Technicians land on their job list instead. See [Job portal and technician access](/delivery/job-portal).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the layout">
    The sidebar on the left holds every page your role can open. Your most-used pages sit at the top: **Inbox**, **Rep Overview**, **Tasks**, **Sales**, **Quotes**, **Customers** and **Comms**. [Find your way around](/reference/workspace) explains each part.

    To jump anywhere, press **⌘K** on a Mac or **Ctrl K** on Windows and type what you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your profile">
    Select your name at the bottom of the sidebar → **Settings**, then **Profile** under **Personal**. Add your photo, personal email and mobile. Then open **Notifications** and choose how alerts reach you.

    Sales reps: set your hours under **Availability** before customers can book you. See [Sales availability](/setup/sales-availability).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow one customer">
    1. Open **Customers → Customer Records** and search for the customer before adding someone new.
    2. Check their contact details, address and assigned rep.
    3. Read their history before you call or text.
    4. Leave a clear next step in **Tasks**, with an owner and a date.

    See [Customer records](/customers/records), [Calls and SMS](/customers/calls-sms) and [Tasks](/customers/tasks).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Practise a quote">
    Select the pencil icon at the top of the sidebar (**Quick Create Quote**), or press **C**. Choose the customer, add their energy use and pick a system. Check the total and the customer preview.

    Practise on a record your manager approves. Stop at the preview: sending a quote contacts a real customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>
