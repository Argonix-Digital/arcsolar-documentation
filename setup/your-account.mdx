## Switch organisation

If you work for more than one business on ArcSolar, one login opens all of them.

1. Select your name at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Select **Switch organisation**.
3. On **Choose a workspace**, select the business you want. Use **Search workspaces** if the list is long.

ArcSolar opens that workspace. You only see the records your role there allows.

<Note>
If you're on a call in Comms, finish the call before you switch.
</Note>

## Delete your account

Deleting your account removes your login, your personal profile and your backup contact details. It can't be undone.

It does **not** erase customer records, contracts, invoices or audit records that belong to the business.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the delete page">
    Go to **Settings** → **Security**. In **Account security**, select **Delete my account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it's you">
    If you haven't signed in recently, ArcSolar asks you to sign in again. Installers can use **Use installer email sign-in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide what happens to businesses you own">
    For each business you own, choose an option under **What should happen?**:
    - **Transfer to an administrator**: pick the **New owner**. Billing and saved payment methods stay with the business.
    - **Close this business workspace**: type `CLOSE`. Every member loses access and the ArcSolar subscription stops renewing. Outstanding charges are still payable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Enter **Your account email**, type `DELETE`, and tick the box. Select **Delete my account**, then **Permanently delete account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the clean-up can't finish straight away, ArcSolar records your request and retries it. You'll see **Your deletion request is processing**. Don't send a second request. Before processing starts you can select **Cancel unprocessed deletion request**.

<Warning>
Own the business? Transfer it to another administrator first if your team will keep using ArcSolar. Closing it locks everyone out.
</Warning>

## "This organisation is closed"

You see this page when a business you belong to has been closed. Nobody can sign in to it, but no data has been deleted. Select **Contact support** if you think it's a mistake.
