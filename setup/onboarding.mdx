This guide is for the **business owner**. It takes about 15 minutes, plus time to load your prices.

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- Your ABN and legal business name
- Your business address, phone and billing email
- A card for your ArcSolar subscription
- Your price list (products and system prices)

## The steps at a glance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.arcsolar.com.au/signup](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/signup). Enter your **Work email** and a **Password** (12 characters or more). Tick the terms box and select **Create account**.

    Open the email from ArcSolar and confirm your address. Then select **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your business details">
    Answer four short screens:

    - **Your name** and date of birth
    - **Your organisation**: name, ABN and legal business name
    - **Where you're based**: your address. ArcSolar sets your timezone from your state
    - **How to reach you**: billing email, business phone, business type and website (optional)

    Select **Create organisation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Pick **Spark** or **Arc**. Arc includes Intelligence and Roof Vision. Open **Calls, SMS and number rates** to see call and SMS pricing.

    Tick the payment authorisation box, then select **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay for your plan">
    Select **Continue to Stripe** and finish checkout. You'll come back to ArcSolar when it's done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on calls and SMS (optional)">
    Select **Enable calls and SMS**, or **Skip for now**. You can do this later. See [Calls and SMS billing](/setup/payto).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take customer payments (optional)">
    Select **Continue to Stripe** to set up deposits and payments from customers, or **Skip for now**. See [Stripe](/integrations/stripe).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your workspace">
    At **Your workspace is ready**, select **Enter workspace**. A short tour shows you around.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only the owner can do these steps. If someone else signs in first, they'll see that the owner is still setting up.
</Note>

## What's next

Your workspace exists, but **you can't quote or call yet**. Finish the four required tasks in the [setup checklist](/setup/checklist) first:

1. STC and quote pricing
2. Confirm your product catalogue
3. Your services and fees
4. Finance and deposits

Then [invite your team](/setup/team) and [bring in your leads](/customers/import-leads).

## If you get stuck

- Closed the page? Sign in again. ArcSolar takes you back to where you stopped.
- Don't create a second account for the same business.
- Stripe checks can take a little while. You can keep setting up in the meantime.
- Still stuck? [Contact support](/reference/support).
