The organisation owner completes the setup. Have the ABN, business details, billing email, phone, address and bank details ready.

This guide follows the documented qa onboarding flow. If your workspace uses an invitation or different prompts, follow those and ask support if you cannot continue.

## Create the workspace

1. Create your account and verify your email.
2. Complete the business profile: identity, address, timezone, services, operating states and team information.
3. Choose **Create organisation**, then review the **Spark** and **Arc** plans and their inclusions.

## Complete billing

| Step | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Communications rates | Choose **Review VoIP rates**, read them and accept separate usage billing |
| Software subscription | Choose **Open Stripe in new tab**, complete checkout and return to ArcSolar |
| Calls and SMS | [Authorise PayTo](/setup/payto) in your banking app and wait for active status; **Skip for now** leaves communications unavailable |
| Customer payments | Complete [Stripe business verification](/integrations/stripe); collection starts when Stripe enables it |

At **Your workspace is ready**, review outstanding items and choose **Enter workspace**.

## If setup stops

Return to the same organisation rather than creating another. Subscription, PayTo and customer-payment setup have separate statuses. Stripe review or bank confirmation can take time. Enter financial and identity details only in the secure setup flow.
