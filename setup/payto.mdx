Calls, SMS and number rentals are billed monthly for what you use, separately from your ArcSolar plan. Only the workspace owner can set this up.

**Open:** [**Settings → Billing**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/settings?tab=organisation-billing) and expand **Phone billing**. You can also do it during [onboarding](/setup/onboarding).

## Choose how to pay

| Option | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Same method as your plan** | Calls and SMS go on the card or bank account (BECS Direct Debit or PayTo) you used for your subscription. Nothing more to enter |
| **PayTo agreement** | A separate monthly PayTo agreement you approve in your banking app |
| **Separate card** | A card used only for calls and SMS |

## Use the same method as your plan

This is the simplest option. You agree to it when you choose your plan.

1. Open **Billing → Phone billing**.
2. Select **Enable calls and SMS**.

Calls and SMS turn on once the payment is confirmed. To change the card or bank account later, select **Manage payment method**. Both your plan and calls and SMS use the new one.

## Set up a PayTo agreement

1. Open **Billing → Phone billing**.
2. Expand **Calls, SMS & number rates** and read the rates.
3. Tick **I accept these rates and separate monthly VoIP billing.**
4. Select **Continue to bank approval**.
5. Approve the agreement in your banking app.

The agreement allows one monthly debit of your actual usage, up to A$10,000. This is a limit your bank approves, not a fee. To change an active agreement, select **Replace mandate with bank approval**.

If a PayTo debit fails, the owner can retry it under **Outstanding phone payments**.

## Use a separate card instead

If PayTo doesn't work for you:

1. Under the PayTo form, select **Having trouble with PayTO?**
2. Select **Continue with Card** and enter the card in the secure payment page.

To change it later, select **Update VoIP billing card**.

## Before you call

Numbers can't be given out until phone billing is active. On **Calls & SMS** you'll see **Complete billing setup** until it is.

Keep these separate:

| Arrangement | Pays for |
| --- | --- |
| Your plan | ArcSolar software. See [Billing](/setup/billing) |
| Phone billing | Calls, SMS and numbers |
| [Stripe customer payments](/integrations/stripe) | Money your customers pay you |
