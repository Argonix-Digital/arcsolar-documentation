Stripe takes your customers' quote deposits and project payments and pays them into your business's Stripe account. Your own ArcSolar subscription and [phone payments](/setup/payto) are separate.

## Set up Stripe

The business owner sets this up, in one of two places:

- **During onboarding:** the **Set up customer payments** step. Select **Continue to Stripe**, or **Skip for now** to do it later.
- **Later:** [**Settings → Billing**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/settings?tab=organisation-billing), open **Customer payment collection** and select **Manage Stripe business details**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Stripe">
Use one of the buttons above. Sign in to your existing Stripe account or create one.
</Step>
<Step title="Submit your business details">
Enter the business, representative, identity and payout bank details Stripe asks for.
</Step>
<Step title="Return to ArcSolar">
Stripe sends you back. While Stripe checks your details, you see: "Stripe is reviewing your payment account."
</Step>
<Step title="Wait for collection to turn on">
When Stripe approves you, **Customer payment collection** says "Your organisation can receive customer payments through its Stripe account." Do not ask a customer to pay before this.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Check or reconnect

Open [**Retailer Settings → Integrations → Stripe**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/quoting-settings?tab=integrations). The card shows **Connected** or **Not connected**, and the account name and ID.

If Stripe needs more details, an Administrator or Operations user can select **Reconnect Stripe account** to open Stripe again. If the link has expired, select it again for a fresh one.

There is no disconnect button. To change the account your payments go to, contact ArcSolar support.

## How a customer pays

1. The customer opens the quote link, accepts the offer and signs the agreement.
2. They pay the amount set by the quote's payment schedule, using the methods Stripe Checkout offers.
3. ArcSolar records the payment against the quote or project when Stripe confirms it.

A payment is only paid once Stripe confirms it. A refund returns money but does not reopen the quote.

If a payment still shows as processing, check the payment record before sending a second link. Never charge twice to fix a delay. Reconcile in [Finance](/delivery/finance).

## Bank transfer fallback

For customers who cannot use card checkout, you can show your bank details instead. Administrators and Finance set this up.

1. Open [**Settings → Customer payments**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/settings?tab=customer-payments).
2. Under **Customer bank transfer**, enter **Account name**, **BSB** and **Account number**.
3. Select **Save bank details**.
4. Check the saved details, tick the confirmation box and select **Confirm saved destination**. Customers only see the details after this step.

Changing the details clears the confirmation. **Disable bank transfer** stops new instructions. A shown bank transfer instruction never marks a deposit as paid. Record the payment once it arrives.
