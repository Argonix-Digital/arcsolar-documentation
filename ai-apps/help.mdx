**Open:** [**Settings → AI apps**](https://app.arcsolar.com.au/dashboard/profile-settings?tab=ai-apps).

## Manage your connections

- **Connected AI apps** lists each app you approved, with its access and organisations. Select **Disconnect** to stop it straight away.
- **Personal access tokens** are listed with their expiry. Select **Revoke** to stop one.
- **Recent AI activity** shows the last 30 things AI apps did with your account, including anything refused.

Disconnecting a connection that covers several organisations ends it in all of them.

## How long a connection lasts

| Connection | Lasts |
| --- | --- |
| App connection (sign-in) | 30 days, then connect again. It also stops if it isn't used for 14 days |
| Personal access token | 7, 30 or 90 days, as chosen |

A connection also ends when:

- You disconnect or revoke it.
- You're suspended or removed from the organisation.
- ArcSolar detects a sign-in token being reused, which can mean it was copied. Activity shows **Blocked a reused sign-in token**. Connect again, and change your password if you didn't expect this.

## Common problems

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| ArcSolar doesn't appear in the app | Turn it on from the chat's tools menu. On Claude Team or Enterprise and ChatGPT business plans, an admin may need to allow the connector first |
| "Permission denied: a … cannot use …" | Your role can't do that in ArcSolar either. Ask an Administrator, or do it in ArcSolar |
| The app can't find a customer or job | It may be assigned to someone else. The app only sees what your role sees |
| Changes are refused | The connection may be **Read only**. Disconnect and connect again with **Read and write** |
| "That preview expired" | Previews last 10 minutes. Ask the app to prepare it again, then confirm |
| "The token is expired or revoked" | Connect again, or create a new personal access token |
| "This connection cannot use that organisation" | You didn't tick that organisation. Connect again and tick it |
| The app asks which organisation to use | On a connection with several organisations, changes must name one. Say "in [organisation name]" |
| "This connection request cannot continue" | Close the window and start the connection again from the AI app |

Error messages end with a short reference. Include it when you contact support.

## Get help

- **From the AI app:** ask it to "report this problem to ArcSolar support". It shows you the report first and sends it only after you confirm. It needs a **Read and write** connection.
- **By email:** send what you asked, what happened and the reference to **[support@arcsolar.com.au](mailto:support@arcsolar.com.au)**. Never include a token or password.

See [Get help from ArcSolar](/reference/support) for other ways to reach us.
