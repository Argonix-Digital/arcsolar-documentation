Connect ArcSolar to an AI app such as Claude or ChatGPT. Then ask about your customers, quotes and jobs, or ask the app to do a task, without opening ArcSolar.

ArcSolar uses the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**, the standard way AI apps connect to other software. Any app that supports remote MCP servers can connect.

<Info>
AI apps are available to every role in every ArcSolar workspace. There's nothing for an administrator to turn on. To connect, see [Connect an AI app](/ai-apps/connect).
</Info>

## It acts as you

- The app can only do what **your role** allows. If you can't see a customer in ArcSolar, the app can't either. See [Roles and permissions](/setup/roles).
- You sign in **in ArcSolar**, never in the AI app. The app never sees your password.
- It works in **one organisation at a time**.
- Every action is recorded against your name. You can see the last 30 in **Settings → AI apps → Recent AI activity**.

## You confirm anything that matters

Looking things up happens straight away. For anything that contacts a customer or a team member, or can't easily be undone, the app first shows you a preview. It only goes ahead after you say yes.

- Previews expire after 10 minutes. Ask again if one expires.
- Bulk price changes run as a dry run first, so you see every change before it's saved.
- A **Read only** connection can look things up but can't change anything.

## What you can ask

| Area | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Customers and leads | "Find Jane Citizen", "Add a solar and battery lead for Sam in Geelong", "Import the leads in this spreadsheet and assign them to me" |
| Quotes | "Where is the deal for Jane Citizen at?", "Did she open the quote?", "Why didn't the quote send?" |
| Payments | "Has the deposit been paid?" (read only) |
| Projects and jobs | "Is this job ready to install?", "Book the install for Friday", "Upload these site photos to the job" |
| Tasks and calendar | "What's on my calendar tomorrow?", "Create a follow-up task for Monday" |
| Products and pricing | "Export my price list", "Update prices from this supplier price list" |
| Reports | "Give me my weekly sales summary", "Show team activity this week" |
| Amy | "Why hasn't Amy called this lead?", "Show Amy's calls from yesterday" |
| Team (Administrators) | "Invite a new sales rep", "Resend Sam's invitation" |
| Help | "How do I import leads?" (answered from these docs), "Report this problem to ArcSolar support" |

## What AI apps can never do

- Change your plan, billing or subscription
- Collect, refund or void a payment
- Change anyone's role, or invite another Administrator
- Buy or release phone numbers
- Change Amy's settings
- Show passwords, tokens or keys
- Send free-form texts or emails to customers
- Permanently delete records
- Anything in ArcLeads

Do these in ArcSolar itself.

## What each role can reach

| Role | What AI apps can do |
| --- | --- |
| Administrator, Operations | Work across your organisation, within your role's permissions |
| Finance | Look across your organisation, including financials. No creating or sending quotes |
| Sales manager | Your own records and your approved team's. No company financials |
| Sales representative | Your own and unassigned customers, your quotes, tasks, calendar and messages, and your own reports. No company financials |
| Installer | Your assigned jobs and uploading site photos. No customers, quotes, messages or financials |
| External marketing | Shared lead outcomes only |

If the app says it can't find a record, it may exist but be outside what your role can see. ArcSolar never confirms records you can't reach.

## Your data

- What the app reads from ArcSolar is sent to the AI app's provider (for example Anthropic or OpenAI) under your agreement with them. Check your company's policy before you connect.
- ArcSolar records which action ran, when and whether it worked. It doesn't store your chat.
- Each connection lasts 30 days. Disconnect it at any time in **Settings → AI apps**.

<Note>
Agent, the chat built into ArcSolar, works the same way inside the app. See [Agent](/agent).
</Note>
